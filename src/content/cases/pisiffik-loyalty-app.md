## The project

The Pisiffik loyalty app is a long-term visual design project that began with the first version of the app in 2021.

Over time, the project grew beyond the interface itself and into a broader customer experience spanning app UI, loyalty communication, customer journeys, campaigns and physical retail touchpoints.

For the major visual update in 2025, I shaped the visual expression across the customer-facing experience. The colour direction was decided by Pisiffik's Marketing Manager; I developed the remaining visual system and carried it across the app and supporting communication.

## My role

I have been the primary graphic designer on the project and have been responsible for the overall visual expression across the customer-facing experience.

My work has covered both digital and physical communication, including app UI design, visual systems, customer journeys, campaigns, promotional communication, launch motion and printed in-store materials.

The technical development and implementation of the app were handled by others.

## Customer journey

A central part of the project was connecting the digital loyalty experience with the physical shopping journey.

The communication had to help customers understand the value of the programme, encourage app downloads and make the benefits relevant in the moments where shopping decisions actually happen.

The journey can be understood as:

- Discover the app in-store
- Download and join
- Use the app during everyday shopping
- Collect and use points
- Receive offers, content and notifications
- Return to the app and the store

This meant designing a connected experience rather than treating the app, campaigns and retail materials as separate pieces.

## App & UI design

The app brings several parts of the Pisiffik customer relationship together in one digital environment.

The interface includes the loyalty points experience, digital membership card, receipts, offers, notifications, campaigns, recipes, games and webshop functionality.

I worked on the visual design and presentation of these customer-facing experiences, creating a consistent interface system with clear hierarchy and a recognisable visual language.

The goal was to make the experience feel useful and easy to navigate while still supporting the commercial role of the app.

## Loyalty & engagement

The loyalty experience needed to give customers a reason to return beyond a single purchase.

Points and member benefits form the core loyalty mechanic, while campaigns, notifications, content and gamified experiences provide additional reasons to open the app.

My role was to shape the visual communication around these moments — from individual screens and offers to the broader system that connects them.

## From screen to store

The app experience was supported by physical communication throughout Pisiffik's stores.

I designed and developed customer-facing materials including hanging signs, in-store signage, shopping bags, checkout communication and other point-of-purchase materials used to introduce and reinforce the loyalty programme.

The objective was simple: the digital and physical experiences should feel like parts of the same system.

A customer should recognise the same message and visual language whether they encounter the programme in an aisle, at checkout or on their phone.

## Launch animation

For the launch of the updated app, I created the opening animation used to introduce the new visual expression before entering the app experience.

The animation translates the visual language of the interface into motion and creates a distinct opening to the updated experience.

## Visual system

The 2025 update established a refreshed visual expression that had to work across very different formats — from small mobile interface elements to large in-store signs and printed materials.

Consistency was important, but so was hierarchy. App interfaces need to remain clear and functional, while retail communication has to compete for attention in a busy environment.

The system was therefore built to balance recognition, clarity and commercial communication across digital and physical touchpoints.

## Scope

The project covered:

- App UI and visual design
- Digital product design
- Loyalty and membership experience
- Customer journeys
- Offers and promotional communication
- Notifications
- Campaigns
- Content and engagement experiences
- Launch motion
- Physical in-store communication
- Shopping bags and POS materials
- Cross-channel visual consistency
