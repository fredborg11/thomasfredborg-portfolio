## The project

The Pisiffik loyalty app is a long-term customer experience project that began with the first version of the app in 2021.

Since then, the experience has evolved across both digital and physical touchpoints. In 2025, the app received a major visual update, creating a clearer and more consistent visual system across the loyalty experience while keeping the core customer proposition focused on points, offers, benefits and everyday shopping.

The project was never only about the app interface. The work extended into the wider customer journey — from getting customers to notice and download the app to giving them reasons to keep using it as part of their everyday shopping experience.

## My role

I have been the primary graphic designer on the project and have been responsible for the overall visual expression across the customer-facing experience.

My work has covered both digital and physical communication, including app interface design, visual systems, customer journeys, campaigns, promotional communication and printed in-store materials.

For the 2025 visual update, the colour direction was decided by Pisiffik's Marketing Manager. I developed the remaining visual expression, including the layout, typography, interface elements, hierarchy and overall design system.

The technical development and implementation of the app were handled by others.

## The customer journey

A central part of the project was connecting the app with the physical shopping experience.

The visual communication needed to help customers understand the value of the loyalty programme, encourage app downloads and make the benefits relevant at the point of purchase.

The journey can be seen as:

- Discover the app in-store
- Download and join
- Use the app during everyday shopping
- Collect and use points
- Receive offers, content and notifications
- Return to the app and the store

The design work therefore had to work across very different moments and formats while still feeling like one connected experience.

## App experience

The app brings several parts of the Pisiffik customer relationship together in one digital environment.

The interface includes the loyalty points experience, digital membership card, receipts, offers, notifications, campaigns, recipes, games and webshop functionality.

I worked on the visual design and presentation of these customer-facing experiences, with a focus on making the different parts feel connected and easy to understand.

## Loyalty & engagement

The loyalty experience was designed to give customers a clear reason to return.

Points and member benefits create the core loyalty mechanic, while campaigns, notifications, content and gamified experiences add additional reasons to open the app.

This meant designing not only the individual screens, but also the visual communication around offers, messages and activities that keep the app relevant between purchases.

## From screen to store

The app experience was supported by physical communication throughout Pisiffik's stores.

I designed and developed customer-facing materials including hanging signs, in-store signage, shopping bags, checkout communication and other point-of-purchase materials used to introduce and reinforce the loyalty programme.

The goal was to make the connection between the physical store and the digital experience clear: the customer should encounter the same idea whether they were walking through the store, standing at the checkout or using the app.

## Visual system

The 2025 update established a refreshed visual expression for the app and its customer-facing communication.

The system was designed to work across very different formats — from small mobile interface elements to large in-store signs and printed materials.

Consistency was important, but so was hierarchy. App interfaces need to remain clear and functional, while retail communication has to compete for attention in a busy environment.

The visual system was therefore built to balance recognition, clarity and commercial communication across both digital and physical touchpoints.

## Scope

The project covered:

- App UI and visual design
- Loyalty and membership experience
- Customer journeys
- Offers and promotional communication
- Notifications
- Campaigns
- Content and engagement experiences
- Physical in-store communication
- Shopping bags and POS materials
- Cross-channel visual consistency
