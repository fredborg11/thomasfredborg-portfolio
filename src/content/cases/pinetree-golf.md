## The project

Pinetree Golf was developed as a new golf brand with the aim of creating a clear and recognizable visual identity that could work consistently across products and digital touchpoints.

I worked across the brand from its core identity through to physical product applications and the original website direction.

## My role

I created the Pinetree Golf logo and developed the visual identity that formed the basis for the brand's communication.

From there, I translated the identity into physical product design, including golf ball graphics and packaging across multiple product variants.

I also developed the original visual direction for the Pinetree Golf website.

## Brand identity

The identity was designed to be simple, recognizable and flexible enough to work across a wide range of applications.

The visual system established the foundation for how Pinetree Golf could appear consistently across products, packaging and digital communication.

## Packaging & product design

The identity was carried through to the physical products, including a range of golf balls and their packaging.

The packaging system was designed around a consistent structure, with individual product variants distinguished through their own colour accents and product-specific details.

This created a unified product family while still making the individual models easy to differentiate.

## Digital direction

I also developed the original visual direction for the Pinetree Golf website, translating the brand identity into a digital environment.

The website was later redesigned after the project moved to another agency. This case presents the original direction and the work created as part of the initial brand development.

## Scope

The project covered:

- Logo design
- Visual identity
- Golf ball design
- Packaging design
- Product variants
- Original website direction
