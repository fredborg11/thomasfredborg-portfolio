## The brief

In 2020, Foreningen af Lystbådehavne i Danmark wanted a new visual direction that could better reflect the organisation and the movement within its industry.

The existing logo was built around an anchor — a recognisable maritime symbol, but one that also gave the identity a feeling of being fixed in place. The ambition was to create something that still felt clearly maritime, while communicating movement and a more contemporary direction.

As part of the project, FLID also wanted to move the production of its member magazine, *Flid og Fakta*, and its wider corporate communication into a more consistent visual system.

## My role

I developed the creative direction from the very beginning and had the primary contact with FLID throughout the graphic and design process.

The project covered:

- Logo development
- Visual identity
- Colour palette
- Typography
- Graphic elements
- Design manual
- Magazine redesign
- Advertisements and print materials
- Website graphics
- Clothing and promotional materials
- Business cards and stationery
- PowerPoint templates
- Roll-ups and posters

From the birth of the logo onwards, I have continued to handle FLID's graphic design across platforms and formats.

## From anchor to movement

The logo development was a long process, with around 10–12 different directions explored before we arrived at the final solution.

The thinking remained rooted in the maritime world, but the challenge was to find a symbol that communicated movement rather than being anchored in place.

The final identity uses a compass-inspired symbol turned slightly away from true north, creating a subtle sense of movement and direction.

The result gave FLID a maritime visual language without relying on the anchor from the previous identity.

## Building a system

The logo was only the beginning.

I developed a design manual covering the logo system, colour palette, typography and supporting graphic elements. The purpose was to create a visual framework that could keep FLID consistent across different formats and suppliers.

The system was designed to be clear enough that other graphic designers could also work within the brand without losing the visual consistency established in the original identity.

## Flid og Fakta

Alongside the identity, I redesigned FLID's existing member magazine, *Flid og Fakta*.

The goal was not to make it feel like a completely different publication. The redesign needed to modernise the magazine while retaining enough of its established character that existing members would still recognise the publication.

I developed a clear layout system that made the magazine easier and less time-consuming to produce. The system established a consistent structure for typography, spacing, imagery and page composition, while remaining flexible enough to handle different types of editorial content.

The magazine is typically around 56 pages and is published four times a year.

I am responsible for the complete design and layout of the publication, while the editorial content is handled by FLID's editor and other contributors. Photography, advertising material, printing and distribution involve external collaborators.

## One identity, many touchpoints

The finished identity was rolled out across FLID's communication, both digitally and in print.

It appears across everything from the website, stationery and presentations to clothing, posters, roll-ups, advertisements and the member magazine.

The identity is still in use today, and I continue to work with FLID on its ongoing graphic production.

## A long-term design partnership

What started as a logo and identity project has developed into an ongoing design partnership.

For me, the project demonstrates the value of building a visual system that is not only distinctive, but also practical to use in the day-to-day communication of an organisation.

It also reflects the breadth of my work as a designer — from developing a brand identity from scratch to creating the systems and layouts that make the identity work in real life, year after year.
